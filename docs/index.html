Zukakito Clips Uploader

Zukakito Clips Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Zukakito - GTA 6 Streamer Clips.

It turns authorised livestream footage from creators' official clipping programs into short vertical clips and uploads them to that one channel through the YouTube Data API, with titles and descriptions that credit the original creator.

The tool does not offer accounts to other people and does not access anyone else's YouTube data.
