# Pre-publish checklist: every Short, every night

_Re-reviewed against each campaign's own brief on 22 Nov 2026; publishing rules updated 27 Nov. Claude runs this on every clip before publishing. Weekdays: publish between 19:00 and 22:00 IST; weekends: checks every 2 h from 12:00 to 20:00 IST, publish as soon as a clip passes (max 3 a day, at least 1 h apart). A clip that fails any line is not published. If a brief changes, update this doc first. Copies: this repo (ops/publish-checklist.md), the owner's Mac (Downloads/zukakito-publishing/), and the Claude project._

## A. Every clip: YouTube and channel
1. **Length and format:** vertical 1080×1920, 30–58 s, plays to the end with no frozen or corrupted frames.
2. **Picture and sound:** sharp (no pixelated facecam), audio and video in sync (within 0.1 s), voice clear, no long silent or black/loading stretches.
3. **Captions:** match what is actually said (spot-check against the audio), swear words bleeped, readable without sound, English only.
4. **Hook and title:** say plainly what happens; no hype or clickbait (epic, insane, mystery, surprise…); no fabricated context; no major story spoilers in the hook or cover.
5. **Community Guidelines:** no sexual content or nudity (strip clubs, lap dances), no graphic gore, no hate or harassment, no real personal information, nothing that mocks or degrades the streamer.
6. **Copyright:** footage only from the campaign's approved streams. After upload, YouTube Studio's **Checks** step must show **no copyright claims** (stream music can trigger Content ID). Any claim means don't publish.
7. **Upload settings:** category Gaming; audience "Not made for kids"; **"Includes paid promotion" ticked** (Content Rewards campaigns are paid); altered/synthetic content "No"; likes visible; added to the streamer's playlist.
8. **No duplicates:** not a near-copy of a clip we already posted from the same part of the stream; never post near-identical clips more than 5 times; each video is submitted to **one campaign only**.
9. **Shown as it happened:** no fake reactions, no edited dialogue that changes meaning, no footage from other streamers, trailers or leaks.
10. **Campaign still payable:** the campaign is open and has budget left. Skip campaigns that have closed or run out.

## C. At publish time
1. **Content Rewards only accepts a clip submitted within 30 minutes of it going live** ("Posted within the last 30 minutes"). So publish only while the owner and the Mac are online, never on an unattended schedule.
2. The owner brings the Claude app to the front ("screen up") and picks the file in YouTube Studio's upload dialog; Claude fills in the settings in A7.
3. Wait for Studio's Checks to finish: no copyright claim.
4. Publish **public immediately**, then submit the link to the clip's one campaign right away.
5. Pin a comment with a short question about the moment.
6. Report back: what was published and submitted, and what was skipped and why.
7. Never delete and re-post a Short to get another submission window: YouTube treats that as spam.
